3. Scroll down and tap on**Settings and Privacy** .
4. Select**Settings** .
5. Tap on**Personal Information** .
6. Select**Contact Info** .
7. To add your new phone number, tap**Add Phone Number** .
8. To add an email address, tap**Add Email Address** .
